Opoplan wireframe preview
Original interface composition and renderer created for Qianhua Ge's portfolio.
Map data: Shinjuku Station Indoor Map Data, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (Japan), adapted into line geometry.
Dataset: https://www.geospatial.jp/ckan/dataset/mlit-indoor-shinjuku-r2
Reference and distributed map data: https://github.com/satoshi7190/Shinjuku-indoor-threejs-demo
Rendering library: Three.js, MIT license.
This asset is a portfolio design preview, not a live Opoplan product deployment.
